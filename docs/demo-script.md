# SkillSetu — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script (SIH26101)

## Scene 1: The Problem & Persona Login (0:00 - 0:40)
- **Presenter:** "Respected Judges, India's Official Statistical System under MoSPI is adopting modern AI, Big Data, and sampling methodologies. But officials struggle to identify their exact skill gaps."
- **Action:** Open `http://localhost:3000/login`. Click **Ananya Sharma (Statistical Officer)** persona login.
- **Show:** The Official Dashboard with the 4-Domain Competency Radar Chart showing initial score (42.0% in Statistical Methods).

## Scene 2: Material Upload & AI Question Generation (0:40 - 1:20)
- **Action:** Switch to **Trainer Persona (Dr. V. K. Rao)**. Open `/trainer/materials`.
- **Action:** Upload `Statistical Methods Training Material.pdf`. Click **Upload & Extract Text**.
- **Action:** Click **Generate AI MCQs**.
- **Action:** Navigate to `/trainer/review` (Human-in-the-Loop) and click **Approve & Publish** on pending MCQs.

## Scene 3: Adaptive Assessment & Visual Transition (1:20 - 2:00)
- **Action:** Switch back to Official Persona. Navigate to `/dashboard/assessment`.
- **Action:** Click **Start Adaptive Quiz**. Answer 5 questions.
- **Action:** Click **Analyze My Competency**.
- **Show:** The visual **"SkillSetu AI Engine: Mapping Competencies & Identifying Knowledge Gaps..."** loading overlay.

## Scene 4: Explainable Gaps & Personalized Training Path (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Show:** The Results page (`/dashboard/results`). Highlight the transparent evidence cards ("Missed 3 of 4 sampling questions").
- **Action:** Click **Generate Personalized Training Path**.
- **Show:** The Personalized Path page (`/dashboard/learning-path`). Point to tagged **NSSTA TPAC** and **iGOT Karmayogi** courses prioritized for Critical Gaps.

## Scene 5: Progress Tracker & Admin Executive Analytics (2:30 - 3:00)
- **Action:** Click **Start Learning** / Mark item completed.
- **Action:** Navigate to `/dashboard/progress`. Show the before/after chart demonstrating **+29 Points Score Improvement**.
- **Action:** Switch to **Admin Persona (Rajesh Kumar)** at `/admin/analytics`.
- **Show:** Department-wide workforce readiness index (64.2% Statistical, 78.5% Technical) and training demand forecasting.
- **Closing:** "SkillSetu bridges competency gaps and creates a future-ready statistical workforce for MoSPI. Thank you!"
